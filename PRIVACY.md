# Privacy policy

Company Highlighter does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **Your company list and settings** (highlight colour, on/off) are saved with the browser's extension storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere.
- **Page content** is read only inside your browser, to find and highlight the names in your list. It is never stored or sent anywhere.
- **No network requests, analytics, tracking or third-party code.**

Uninstalling the extension deletes its stored list and settings.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Ahmed-Manai/company-highlighter-browser-extension/issues
