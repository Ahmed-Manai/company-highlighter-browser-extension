# Store listing

Text for the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons submission forms.

## Name
Company Highlighter

## Summary (132 characters max)
Highlight the companies from your own list on any web page: job boards, LinkedIn, news and search results. Private and local.

## Description
Scanning job boards, LinkedIn feeds, news or search results for the companies you care about? Company Highlighter marks them for you.

Add your list once (target employers, clients, competitors, or companies to avoid) and every occurrence is highlighted on every page you visit.

FEATURES
• Paste your list, or import and export a .txt file
• Case-insensitive, whole-name matching: "Acme" does not match "Acmeish"
• Works with names like AT&T or C++, and prefers the longest name ("Acme Labs" over "Acme")
• Keeps up with infinite scroll and single-page apps
• Match count on the toolbar icon
• Choose your highlight colour, switch it on or off in one click
• Handles lists with thousands of names

PRIVACY
Your list stays in your browser. No network requests, no tracking, no analytics.

Open source: https://github.com/Ahmed-Manai/company-highlighter-browser-extension

## Category
Productivity (Chrome) / Search Tools or Productivity (Firefox)

## Single purpose (Chrome)
Highlights company names from a user-provided list on web pages.

## Permission justifications (Chrome)
- **storage:** stores the user's company list and settings locally.
- **Host permissions / content script on all sites:** the extension must read page text on the sites the user visits to find and highlight the names in their list. Text is processed locally and never transmitted.

## Data usage (Chrome)
Does not collect or use any user data. Privacy policy: https://github.com/Ahmed-Manai/company-highlighter-browser-extension/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
